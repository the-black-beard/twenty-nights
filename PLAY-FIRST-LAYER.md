# Twenty Nights — first Nether layer

Local TN-D2 trial · 20 October 2026 · provisional for Igby's feedback.

## Start

Open [Twenty-Nights-Nether-First-Layer.html](Twenty-Nights-Nether-First-Layer.html) in a browser and choose **Play the Nether layer**. It is a self-contained offline file. **Play the original woods** plays the copied original game; completing Night 20 enters this Nether layer with the woods checkpoint preserved.

## Play

1. Hold and drag on the board to move. Lifting your finger or mouse finishes the current route. Dodge is on. Arrow keys / WASD also work.
2. Find the golden pickaxe near the upper left. Red brick requires this upgrade.
3. Tap any reachable red brick to walk beside it and mine. Mining takes 1.8 seconds; tap ground to cancel and escape. The opening stays and lets the pursuer through too.
4. Reach the green **EXIT** inside the brick ring to clear this layer.

The blue refuge is safe. Purple void and catches each cost **one life**. You have **three lives total**: the initial try and two returns. Returns retain your upgrade, openings and released pursuer; they do not reset the danger. The third loss ends the attempt. There is no countdown.

**Pause** or Space / Escape stops play. **Save & menu**, then **Continue saved Nether trial**, keeps the same attempt and remaining lives. Continue opens paused. Autosave needs browser storage; watch the footer. If it says saving is unavailable, keep the tab open. Moving or renaming the HTML, switching browser/profile or clearing browser data can separate or remove its save.

**Play the Nether layer** starts fresh and replaces this trial's saved attempt. The end screen also offers a fresh replay. Trial saves use separate keys from the original game's saves. Keep this copy in its own folder.
