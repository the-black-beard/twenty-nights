# Twenty Nights

A woods survival game designed by ig-b-dynamix (8) with his dad. You arrive in the woods
one morning, you have twenty nights to live through, and the only thing that
truly matters is what happens to your Home.

This glossary is the shared language. Where the code still uses an older word
for something, the word here wins.

## Time

**Night**:
One cycle of darkness in which monsters attack. The game is exactly twenty of
them, numbered 1–20.
_Avoid_: round, wave, level

**Wave**:
The set of monsters sent at a single Night. A Night has one Wave.
_Avoid_: spawn, group

**Pip**:
The record of how one Night went, shown as a coloured square: cleared it,
rescued by sunrise, Home was hit, or Home was lost.
_Avoid_: star, score, badge

**Dawn Rescue**:
Sunrise ending a Night while monsters are still alive. You survive, but you did
not clear it, and the Pip says so.
_Avoid_: timeout, survival

**Wild Night**:
One of the six Nights with altered conditions — fog, a blood moon, a storm, an
unnaturally still night, deep dark, or a long one.
_Avoid_: event, modifier, special

## The place

**Home**:
The single tile you are defending. Losing it is the only real loss in the game.
_Avoid_: heart, base, core, nest

**Portal**:
A fixed point at the edge of the woods that Waves walk out of.
_Avoid_: spawner, gate

**Nest**:
A mound that appears near a Portal and grows each morning you leave it alone,
eventually becoming a Portal of its own, right beside you.
_Avoid_: hive, den

**Village**:
Where the Neighbours live. Reachable by day, and the only place to trade.
_Avoid_: town, market

**Neighbour**:
A villager who will swap things with you — the farmer, the mason, the tinker.
_Avoid_: NPC, trader, merchant

**Great Lantern**:
The long project you can choose to feed wood into across the twenty Nights. It
is the game's optional ambition, not a requirement.
_Avoid_: beacon, tower

## What comes for you

**Chewer**:
The monster that eats through what you have built rather than walking around it.
_Avoid_: digger, muncher, breaker

**Big One**:
The armoured monster. A stone sword cannot hurt it while its plates hold.
_Avoid_: brute, tank, boss

**Rusher**:
The fast, fragile monster that arrives well ahead of the rest of its Wave.
_Avoid_: runner, sprinter

**Flyer**:
The monster that ignores everything you have built and travels in a straight
line. It must be met in person.
_Avoid_: bird, bat, ghost

**Snatcher**:
The monster that steals rather than fights, and runs for the edge with it.
_Avoid_: thief, robber

## How it works

**Route**:
The path monsters take from a Portal to your Home. Everything you build is a way
of making that Route longer, not of stopping it.
_Avoid_: path, flow field, pathing

**Light Maze**:
Steering monsters by placing light rather than walls. They will cross light, but
will walk a good way around it if they can.
_Avoid_: torch wall, light barrier

**Clang**:
A sword bouncing off a Big One's armour. It does no damage, and the game says
so loudly rather than pretending otherwise.
_Avoid_: block, parry, miss

**Squabble**:
Two monsters travelling opposite ways colliding and fighting each other. ig-b-dynamix's
idea. Each monster can only do it once a Night.
_Avoid_: infighting, friendly fire

**Gnaw**:
A Chewer eating one of your structures. Each Chewer does this to exactly one
structure per Night and no more.
_Avoid_: damage, chewing, destruction

**Wreck**:
What is left of a structure a Chewer has eaten. It stays wrecked until you mend
it, and it still costs you.
_Avoid_: rubble, ruin, debris

**Fort Tier**:
How large what you have built has grown, which is what decides how many Chewers
the woods send. Building more is a choice with a price, and the game tells you
the price out loud.
_Avoid_: level, difficulty, score

**Brag Card**:
The pasteable summary of a run — the twenty Pips as coloured squares, plus what
you achieved. How a player shows a friend what happened.
_Avoid_: scorecard, share text
