# The forgiving contract

ig-b-dynamix was six when this started, and the game was built on a promise: the day
always advances, Home is the only thing you can really lose, and you never lose
everything. A bad Night costs you a Pip and some rebuilding, never your run.

We chose this over a conventional fail-and-restart loop because a seven-year-old
who loses twenty minutes of work stops playing, and because the interesting
decisions in this game are about where to build, not about whether you dare to.

**Consequences**: there is no game-over screen, Night 21 always arrives, and
difficulty has to come from somewhere other than the threat of losing. See
ADR-0006. Any proposal to add real failure is a reversal of this decision and
should be taken deliberately, with ig-b-dynamix, rather than slipped in as a feature.
